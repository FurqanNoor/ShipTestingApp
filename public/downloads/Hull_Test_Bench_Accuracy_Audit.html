Main findings

CRITICAL

1. Open and printed hull displacement can be wrong by most of the actual volume.

A 200 × 60 × 40 mm thin-wall basin should exclude 480 cm³ of water when dry up to its rim. The engine returns 62.912 cm³: exactly the plastic-wall material volume, 86.9% too low. An open flat-bottom exterior skin returns zero instead of 480 cm³. Vertically separated shells are bridged, adding a false 50% displacement in one fixture.

A051–A053; src/engine/hull.ts:32–95
CRITICAL

2. The leaderboard cannot yet support defensible team placements.

An oversized hull can win. A statically stable load that capsizes in waves still counts fully in the advertised one-third-per-failed-load factor. Nonfinite or zero drag can propagate NaN scores. Changing settings while sequential retests run can compare or export old and new conditions together, because results have no immutable protocol snapshot.

A095, A097, A098, A100; src/engine/analyze.ts:195–218; audit/interface-review.md
HIGH

3. Drag has a specific coefficient error and unvalidated model-scale assumptions.

The Holtrop run-length term divides the already-percent LCB by 100 again. Selected reference cases show +1.46% and −2.75% form-factor discrepancies; these are not total-drag error bounds. Default model Re is about 87,649, yet fully turbulent ITTC-57 friction is used without a per-result applicability flag. Clamped coefficients and invalid inputs can still produce plausible output.

A070–A074, A080; src/engine/drag.ts:41–50
HIGH

4. Capsize, flooding and boundary statements exceed what is actually calculated.

Open-rim GZ after deck immersion assumes the virtually sealed hull, without water ingress. A 90° sweep does not establish a capsize angle beyond 90°. The tank checks are preliminary heuristics using default hull dimensions; no wall/reflection/finite-depth flow or domain-convergence study is solved. Calm rows can say Dry despite an immersed rim.

A021; audit/interface-review.md; src/ui/main.ts:197–207,240–260
HIGH

5. Real wave-response accuracy remains unmeasured.

CG defaults to the envelope-volume centroid. Actual wall thickness, ballast, cargo CG, roll inertia, added mass and damping are not measured. One benign time-step check changes predicted roll by 0.52%, but this only tests numerical convergence. A single short, deterministic irregular-wave realization cannot certify extreme roll or capsize probability.

A088–A092; src/engine/sim.ts:99–150; audit/simulation-method-review.md
MEDIUM

6. Automatic geometry assumptions and grid errors need explicit checks.

The tested positive-up axis rotations recover correctly; all 12 inverted-up rotations retain an upside-down keel. A known 20 mm file is guessed as centimetres. At the exact production fine-grid resolution, a 220 × 37 × 16.5 mm box has +2.196% capacity error. An open V prism is 3.33% low. These examples do not bound errors on arbitrary STL files.

A040–A047, A055; src/engine/orient.ts:99–150

The 100 additional simulations

Five hull forms × five fractions of reconstructed capacity × calm plus three default wave presets. The beam-wave runs use the app's production dynamic-grid resolution and 40 peak periods. This stress matrix uses capacity fractions, rather than the application's three gram presets.

All 100 runs produce finite traces and stay within the selected displacement-force bound; no duration cap is hit. However, 20 initially unstable cases capsize before measurement begins, so their fallback mg force extrema are not post-startup measurements. There are32 wet-deck cases, including12 initially stable cases. Flooding is not solved.

Before using placements

  1. Validate the actual exterior displacement envelope of your STL exports.
  2. Snapshot conditions, enforce size eligibility and agreed failure penalties, and reject nonfinite scores.
  3. Correct the LCB coefficient calculation and report drag-method assumptions.
  4. Use measured loaded mass and CG, characterize inertia/damping, and stop physical interpretation at flooding or the evaluated angle limit.
  5. Calibrate against repeatable tow/wave-tank measurements or validated CFD, and quantify uncertainty relative to the differences between designs.
